# Project Atlas kickoff preparation

Document version: 1
Prepared: 24 October 2026
Owner: Product Operations
Status: Fictional planning note

The Project Atlas kickoff is planned for 30 October 2026 at 10:00 am Sydney time. Product Operations will lead the meeting. IT and Workplace Operations will attend as the owners of access setup and office information.

The meeting has four goals. First, confirm that the pilot remains limited to 50 Sydney volunteers for six weeks. Second, review the current sign-in and content-approval status. Third, agree how an unanswered employee question will reach the correct support team. Fourth, record the remaining actions with an owner and due date.

The proposed agenda is:

1. Product Operations explains the pilot goal and measures of success.
2. IT reports how many volunteer accounts have been tested and identifies any access blockers.
3. Workplace Operations presents the Sydney office information for approval.
4. The three owners walk through one example of an unanswered employee question and its support route.
5. Product Operations reviews the action list and records any new decisions.

The kickoff is a planning meeting; its scheduled date does not mean the pilot is ready to launch. Readiness must be checked against the charter and the latest status update. If an action remains open, the project tracker should record its description, owner, due date, and the source document that identified it. An action is complete only when a later status update confirms completion.
