# Project Atlas charter

Document version: 1
Owner: Product Operations
Status: Fictional pilot

Project Atlas is a six-week pilot of an internal employee self-service portal for the Sydney office. The pilot includes 50 volunteer employees. Its purpose is to help employees find approved onboarding, IT access, and workplace information without searching across several document libraries.

The kickoff meeting will confirm the pilot scope, owners, milestones, and measures of success. Product Operations owns the pilot plan. IT owns access and identity setup. Workplace Operations owns office information. Each team must review the information it contributes before the pilot begins.

The pilot is ready to launch when the three owners have approved their content, all 50 volunteers can sign in, and the support route for unanswered questions has been tested. The assistant must not claim that these conditions are complete unless a current project document explicitly confirms them.

Success will be measured by whether volunteers can find the right information, whether answers identify their source, and how often a question needs to be passed to a team. The kickoff should produce a named owner and due date for every open action.
